How it works

1

Photograph the item

One photo on any surface. The cast shadow is what matters, so daylight works best.

2

Silhouette to 64 numbers

The dark region is isolated, normalised and turned into a descriptor that ignores colour.

3

Ranked candidates

Cosine similarity ranks the found reports. You send a claim; the finder approves it.
